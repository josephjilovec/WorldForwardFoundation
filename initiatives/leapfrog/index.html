THE LEAPFROG PROJECT

Skipping Decades of Legacy Infrastructure Through Solar, Satellite, and AI.

Developing communities do not need to wait 30 years for legacy power grids and copper internet cables. The Leapfrog Project deploys containerized solar microgrids, satellite connectivity, and offline-first AI tutoring nodes—allowing remote schools and communities to move directly into modern educational and technical capabilities.

THE OPERATING THESIS

Build around what can be deployed now.

Instead of reproducing every legacy infrastructure layer in sequence, a pilot can combine modular generation, local storage, decentralized water production, edge computing, and periodic satellite synchronization. The system is designed around local installation, local maintenance training, and phased handoff—not permanent external operation.

01

Off-Grid Energy & Water

Modular solar arrays paired with storage and atmospheric water generation or context-appropriate filtration systems, installed and serviced with local trade contractors.

Local execution layer

Electrical installation, mounting, civil work, plumbing, maintenance, replacement planning, and regional procurement.

02

Offline-First AI Edge Nodes

Pre-loaded hardware units running localized open-source AI tutoring engines over a local Wi-Fi mesh, so core learning functions remain available even when the wide-area internet connection is intermittent.

Education layer

Localized curriculum, teacher controls, device management, safeguarding, progress storage, and vocational learning modules.

03

Hybrid Satellite Connectivity

High-speed satellite links such as Starlink can periodically synchronize student progress, retrieve updated learning modules, support administration, and connect communities to global services where local regulation and service availability permit.

Connectivity layer

Bandwidth policy, secure sync windows, network filtering, account administration, power resilience, and alternative providers where appropriate.

Deployment standard

The named technologies are reference architectures, not claims of an existing vendor partnership or active field deployment. Final pilots must be engineered around local law, climate, connectivity availability, water conditions, safeguarding, maintenance capacity, and lifecycle cost.
