THE LEAPFROG PROJECT

Skip legacy infrastructure.

Containerized solar microgrids, off-grid water systems, satellite connectivity, and offline-first AI learning nodes can give remote communities access to capabilities that otherwise require decades of conventional infrastructure buildout.
