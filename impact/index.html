FIVE-STAGE IMPACT PIPELINE

From committed capital to permanent local operation.

1

Co-Investment & Governance

Secure regional matching capital, defined decision rights, documented procurement, and public-ledger auditability appropriate to the project.

2

Local Economic Multiplier

Track direct procurement from native suppliers, local labor participation, contractor development, and money retained in the regional economy.

3

Direct Reach

Verify concrete outputs such as individuals housed, clean-water volume produced, connected households, or students using Leapfrog learning hubs.

4

Measurable Outcomes

Evaluate whether delivery translates into data-driven gains such as literacy, job placement, attendance, income stability, or disease reduction.

5

Permanent Durability

Complete operational handoff to trained local operators, funded maintenance structures, and self-sustaining cooperatives or institutions.
